ℹ️ How It Works

This system uses Content-Based Filtering with Cosine Similarity to recommend mobile phones based on your preferences.

similarity(user, phone) = (A · B) / (|A| × |B|)

Each phone is represented as a normalized feature vector: [storage, RAM, performance, main_camera, selfie_camera, battery, screen_size]

Your slider values form a preference vector. We compute cosine similarity between your vector and each phone in the filtered (budget + OS) candidate set.

A brand bonus (+5%) is applied when a phone matches your preferred brand.

Dataset: Kaggle — Cellphones Recommendations (meirnizri) · 33 phones · 13 features

Tech stack: Python · Pandas · scikit-learn · joblib · Flask
